How scoring works

Every square that holds a piece in either the real position or your answer counts. Your accuracy is the share of those squares you got exactly right. Points earned are accuracy × 10 × n, doubled for a perfect solve. Consecutive perfect solves build your streak.

Placing pieces: drag them from the palettes, or click a palette piece and then click squares. Drag a piece off the board, right-click it, or use the eraser to remove it.
